Question 9 · The essentials

Three principles,
one flexible system.

01

Fluid Layouts

Let columns and spacing expand or contract with the available space.

width: min(90vw, 1100px);
02

Flexible Media

Keep images inside their containers so they never break the composition.

img { max-width: 100%; }
03

Breakpoints

Change the layout when the content needs a new arrangement.

@media (max-width: 600px)
