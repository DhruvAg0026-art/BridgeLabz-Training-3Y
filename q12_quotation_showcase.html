Words of Wisdom

On Perseverance

"Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts."

— Winston Churchill

On Simplicity

"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication."

— Leonardo da Vinci

On Dreams

"The future belongs to those who believe in the beauty of their dreams."

— Eleanor Roosevelt

Quotes inspire people because they distill complex life lessons into simple, memorable words. They offer perspective in moments of doubt, motivate action, and remind us that others have walked similar paths before finding success.
