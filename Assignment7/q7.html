Reading room

Typography gives ideas a shape

Good type creates a rhythm that helps a reader stay with a thought.

Typography is more than choosing a font. It is the relationship between weight, scale, and space. A carefully placed highlight can guide attention, while emphasis can change the tone of a sentence.

Readable text gives every line enough room to breathe. When the details are balanced, the words feel clear before the reader consciously notices why.
