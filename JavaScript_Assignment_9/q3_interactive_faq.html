Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to toggle its answer. Double-click any question to collapse all answers.

1. How do I create an account?

Click "Sign Up", fill in your details and verify your email.

2. How can I reset my password?

Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page to receive a reset email.

3. What payment methods are supported?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking and cash on delivery.

4. How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express takes 1-2 days.

5. What is the return policy?

You can return any item within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.
